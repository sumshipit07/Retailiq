## 1. Product Definition

Retailiq is a role-based store rating platform where users can discover stores and submit ratings from 1 to 5.

The platform supports three roles:

- System Administrator — manages users, stores, and platform-level information.
- Normal User — registers, browses stores, and submits or modifies ratings.
- Store Owner — views ratings and the average rating for their store.

The goal is to build a simple, secure, responsive, and user-friendly web application that demonstrates authentication, role-based access, store management, and rating functionality.
